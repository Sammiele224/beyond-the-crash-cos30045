How should I read these numbers?

These are hospitalised injury counts, not a person’s chance of injury. Group sizes and travel exposure differ. “Remoteness” means usual residence, not crash location.

The site uses a public BITRE snapshot for 2011–2021, checked against national exports in the draft KNIME workflow. The team must verify its final export before assessment.
